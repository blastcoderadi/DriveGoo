2. Core Business Logic Highlights

Date Overlap Availability

A booking conflicts iff newPickup < existing.return AND newReturn > existing.pickup. Adjacent rentals (pickup on the same day as previous return) are permitted. Protected by @Lock(PESSIMISTIC_WRITE) against simultaneous double-booking.

Strict Server-Side Pricing

The frontend is never trusted for monetary amounts. Every total is recalculated on the backend: Total = (dailyPrice × days) + categoryCharge + 5% GST + deposit. All money calculations use BigDecimal with RoundingMode.HALF_UP.

Unified Authentication

Single "Continue" flow supports Google OAuth2 login and Phone OTP. OTPs are 6-digit codes stored as BCrypt hashes with 5-minute expiry and a 5-attempt limit.

Admin Fleet Management

Role-based authorization prevents customers from accessing /admin/**. Admins can approve/reject bookings, toggle vehicle availability, record late returns, and audit payments.
